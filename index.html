About me

I don’t just work with data; I explore it to uncover patterns, insights, and stories that can support smarter decisions and practical solutions.

My journey in Artificial Intelligence is primarily focused on Data Analysis, with a growing interest in Machine Learning and AI-powered tools. I enjoy working with data from the ground up—from collecting and cleaning raw datasets to exploring patterns, analyzing trends, creating meaningful visualizations, and extracting actionable insights.

My main focus areas include:

Data Analysis

Cleaning, transforming, exploring, and analyzing datasets to discover meaningful patterns, trends, and insights.

Data Visualization

Turning complex data into clear and effective visualizations that make information easier to understand and communicate.

Machine Learning

Applying machine learning algorithms to data-driven problems, including building models that can learn from historical data and make predictions.

Prompt Engineering & AI Tools

Using effective prompting and AI-powered tools to simplify workflows, accelerate analysis, improve productivity, and support different stages of the data and machine learning process.

I believe that data becomes truly valuable when it can be transformed into understandable insights and practical solutions. That’s why I follow a hands-on, project-based approach, continuously improving my technical skills through real-world projects and experimentation.

I’m passionate about learning, building, documenting my progress, and exploring how Data Analysis, Machine Learning, and AI can work together to solve real-world problems.

Let’s connect! I’m always open to learning, sharing knowledge, and collaborating on data-driven and AI projects.
